Counter Verification Guide

1. The 3-Visit Loyalty Rule

Guests earn 1 star per visit automatically. You only verify and redeem a code when they unlock an IMPACT- reward on their 3rd visit.

2. Physical Receipt Check

If an offer states "Valid Today Only," verify that the 4-digit Bill Number on screen matches the guest's physical paper receipt.

3. Fixing Honest Typos (Edit Button)

If the guest mistyped a digit of their bill number, tap Edit next to the bill number to update it before redeeming.

4. Confirming Redemption

After tapping Confirm Redemption, mark an 'X' on the guest's physical receipt so it cannot be reused.

5. Flagging Fake / Spam

Only use Flag as Fake / Spam if a guest presents a forged receipt or abuses the system. This blacklists their email.
